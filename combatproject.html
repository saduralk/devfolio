    Melee Combat

  • Presses input, Gameplay Ability "Axe Combo" begins.
  • Animation montage begins--only continues if presses again.
  • An AnimNotify displays the Niagra particle.
  • Multi-sphere trace by channel. follows the axe's motion and applies Gameplay Effect Damage via Gameplay Tag.
  • On death, the actor is lifted and ragdolls, disabling collision.

    Ranged Combat

  • For Gameplay Ability Dash, a niagra particle is simply spawned and the player is moved forward on activation.
  • Gameplay Ability Area of Effect Attack has two phases: targeting and attacking.
  • AI simply finds player's location for target.
  • Player is given control over targeting.
  • Targeted area is highlighted with a decal.
  • Niagra particle is spawned, multisphere trace by channel appears, damage is applied via Gameplay Tags to each actor.
  • For Gameplay Ability Ranged Attack - Shoot Projectile, an animation notify plays on the animation montage.
  • It spawns a projectile, moving directly ahead of the camera.
